# Mallitalytics X Brand Soul

The decision framework for `@Mallitalytics`. Read this before making a Mallitalytics-facing content, product, copy, visual, UX, metric, or editorial decision.

Last updated: August 2026

---

## Scope

This document governs the Mallitalytics baseball brand: MLB, LIDOM, baseball analysis, the website, articles, social posts, cards, data products, and original metrics such as MalliScore.

Mallitalytics is baseball-first. Do not position the account as a general-sports brand or introduce non-baseball coverage unless the user explicitly changes that direction.

This document defines what the brand means. `MALLITALYTICS_BRAND.md` defines how the brand looks.
`MALLITALYTICS_ARTICLE_SOUL.md` governs long-form article research, structure, drafting,
and review.

---

## Core Essence

**Look closer.**

Baseball becomes more beautiful when you look closer. Mallitalytics helps fans find the layer beneath the obvious result: a pattern, tradeoff, pitch shape, player tendency, or context that changes the read of the game.

The brand is not built to publish more data. It is built to help people see baseball differently.

## Brand Stack

| Role | Official language | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| Public slogan | **The game has more to say.** | Main brand line for the X account, website, and major brand moments. Do not force it onto every graphic. |
| Audience invitation | **See what the game is saying.** | Use for homepage, pinned posts, series introductions, and calls to explore. |
| Method | **The signal behind the score.** | Use when explaining the analytical approach, a product, or MalliScore. |
| Internal promise | **Mallitalytics finds the signal in baseball and makes it clear enough to matter.** | Decision standard for agents and internal planning; do not treat it as a mandatory public tagline. |
| Founder belief | **Baseball becomes more beautiful when you look closer.** | Internal truth that should shape the tone of long-form work and major storytelling. |

The former phrase **"béisbol analítico sin opiniones vacías"** remains an editorial standard, not the primary public slogan.

## Audience Value

Mallitalytics helps curious baseball fans notice the details that make the game more interesting, then explains why they matter.

Each piece should aim to provide at least one of these outcomes:

- **Discovery:** "I had not noticed that."
- **Clarity:** "Now I understand why that happened."
- **Anticipation:** "Now I know what to watch next."

These are audience outcomes, not a content-production checklist. Use the editorial operating system below to create them.

Do not confuse the founder's fantasy-baseball origin with the entire audience promise. Fantasy baseball taught the Mallitalytics lens, but the brand serves any curious fan who wants to understand and enjoy baseball more deeply.

---

## Editorial Operating System

For analysis, storytelling, featured cards, and article ideas, work through:

> **NOTICE -> EXPLAIN -> WATCH**

This is the Mallitalytics production system. It is the required workflow for shaping analysis; **Discovery -> Clarity -> Anticipation** describes the value the finished piece should create for the fan.

| Step | Question |
|---|---|
| Notice | What should the fan see that they may not have noticed before? |
| Explain | What evidence and baseball context make it meaningful? |
| Watch | What should the fan watch next to confirm, challenge, or deepen the read? |

### Content Modes

**Analysis and editorial content** must have a clear Notice moment. Before publishing, complete this sentence:

> What I want the fan to notice is: ________.

**Utility content** such as schedules, starters, clean leaderboards, search, and databases does not need a manufactured thesis. Its standard is different:

> Does this help the fan find or act on useful baseball information quickly?

When utility becomes a featured social post or editorial graphic, add a genuine Notice moment. Never invent one merely to make a routine update sound profound.

## Integrity Rules

Mallitalytics earns trust by treating data as evidence, not decoration.

- Do not ship empty opinions, unexplained stat dumps, or hype without evidence.
- Do not make causal claims that the data cannot support. State uncertainty when it matters.
- Treat MalliScore and every original metric as a useful lens, not unquestionable truth.
- Favor the tension in a player or game over a simplistic narrative: a real strength can coexist with a real risk.
- Use numbers to make the game more legible and interesting, never to make the audience feel excluded.
- Be precise, curious, conversational, and baseball-native. Do not become cold, loud, or hot-take driven.

---

## Decision Gate For Agents

Before shipping or recommending a Mallitalytics-facing decision, answer:

1. Does this serve a curious baseball fan?
2. Is the value discovery, clarity, anticipation, or utility?
3. If it is analysis, what is the Notice moment?
4. Is the evidence adequate for the claim?
5. Does the work make baseball more interesting or easier to understand?

If the answer to the last question is no, revise the work or do not ship it.

## Relationship To Visual Branding

Apply `MALLITALYTICS_BRAND.md` after this document. Its visual system should support the content's point rather than compete with it:

- Data is evidence; interpretation gives the evidence meaning.
- A chart or graphic should add context rather than repeat a caption.
- Branding should be recognizable but never overpower the baseball insight.
