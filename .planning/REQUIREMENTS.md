# Requirements: Mallitalytics Showcase

**Defined:** 2026-08-10  
**Core Value:** Every public piece helps a curious fan find the signal behind the score and makes it clear enough to matter.

## v1 Requirements

### Public Reading & Discovery

- [ ] **PUB-01**: A visitor can use the home page to reach featured analysis, recent work, selected visual work, and at least one intentional archive path.
- [ ] **PUB-02**: A visitor can open a stable, shareable detail page for every published article, card, study, project, tracker, or selected social item.
- [ ] **PUB-03**: A visitor can browse published work through small, editorially meaningful content-type archives.
- [ ] **PUB-04**: A visitor can find published work by title or controlled metadata, with archive filters as a usable fallback.
- [ ] **PUB-05**: A visitor only receives content whose publication status is public and whose scheduled publication time has arrived.

### Editorial Value & Trust

- [ ] **EDIT-01**: Every featured analytical item communicates a real Notice moment, supporting evidence and context, and a meaningful Watch-next implication.
- [ ] **EDIT-02**: Utility content helps a visitor find or act on useful baseball information quickly without a manufactured analytical thesis.
- [ ] **EDIT-03**: An analytical item can display its relevant data window, source or methodology, and caveat when needed to interpret the claim responsibly.
- [ ] **EDIT-04**: A visitor can find brand-led About, methodology, corrections, contact, and non-betting editorial-position information.

### Curation & Content Model

- [ ] **CUR-01**: The author can maintain a curated launch inventory that records whether each existing source item is publishable, held, rejected, or missing required editorial metadata.
- [ ] **CUR-02**: Every published launch item has a title, summary, publication date, content type, controlled topic, stable slug, and optional player/team association only where supported by the content.
- [ ] **CUR-03**: Public taxonomy pages and filters appear only when their curated content coverage is sufficient to make them useful.

### Visual Reading & Sharing

- [ ] **VIS-01**: A visitor can read a visual card or chart on a mobile device with descriptive alt text, caption/context, and an enlarged or dedicated visual view when needed.
- [ ] **VIS-02**: Every published detail page exposes a canonical URL and social-preview metadata appropriate for direct sharing.

### Private Author Studio

- [ ] **STU-01**: The author can create, edit, and revision-preview a content item without changing website code.
- [ ] **STU-02**: The author can add or select a visual asset and record its alt text, caption, source, and method notes before publication.
- [ ] **STU-03**: The author can explicitly publish, unpublish, archive, and correct an item while preserving its revisions and preventing drafts from leaking publicly.

## v2 Requirements

### Editorial Expansion

- **COLL-01**: The author can create an ordered, cross-format editorial collection from selected published work.
- **TRACK-01**: A recurring tracker can show declared update cadence, freshness state, update history, and archival snapshots.
- **REL-01**: A visitor can navigate from an item to related work through proven taxonomy or curated associations.

### MLB Ops-Assisted Publishing

- **AUTO-01**: MLB Ops can submit one supported content type as a private candidate with provenance, checksum, and validation results.
- **AUTO-02**: The author can approve or reject an MLB Ops candidate in the studio before it can become published content.

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Subscription, paywall, payment, or premium funnel | The current goal is public visibility, useful baseball work, and audience building rather than monetization. |
| Reader accounts, comments, forums, or social-feed mechanics | Adds moderation and product complexity that do not serve a one-author publication. |
| Betting picks, odds conversion, affiliate calls to action, or performance promises | Dilutes the independent evidence-led editorial position. |
| Live-score product or full baseball-statistics platform | Data-freshness and operational commitments would overwhelm the curated-publication mission. |
| Blind import of all historical MLB Ops outputs | A large uncurated dump weakens discovery, metadata quality, and first impressions. |
| Unreviewed automated publication | Generated work must remain a reviewable private candidate until the author approves it. |
| Generic résumé/CV experience | Mallitalytics should lead as a brand and publication; the work itself proves the craft. |
