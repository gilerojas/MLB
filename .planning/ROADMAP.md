# Roadmap: Mallitalytics Showcase

## Overview

Mallitalytics will become a useful, brand-led baseball publication before it becomes a professional showcase: a curated body of work that lets curious fans notice a signal, understand the evidence, and know what to watch next. The MVP moves from a deliberately selected public collection, through responsible visual reading and a safe manual author workflow, to a launch-ready archive and trust surface. The public implementation stays in `mallitalytics-public/`; `.planning/` remains root documentation. MLB Ops-assisted candidate ingest is a later V2 milestone, after manual editorial publishing has proven the contract and review process.

## Phases

**Phase Numbering:**
- Integer phases are planned MVP work.
- Decimal phases are reserved for urgent insertions.

- [ ] **Phase 1: Curated public reading** - Visitors can explore a deliberately selected launch collection through a real editorial home, detail pages, and meaningful archives.
- [ ] **Phase 2: Responsible visual and editorial context** - Readers can interpret analytical and utility work responsibly, including accessible visual evidence.
- [ ] **Phase 3: Manual editorial publishing** - The author can safely create, revise, and control public content without changing website code.
- [ ] **Phase 4: Discovery, trust, and launch readiness** - Visitors can find, share, and evaluate the publication through complete discovery and trust paths.

## Phase Details

### Phase 1: Curated public reading
**Goal**: Curious baseball fans can enter a coherent, curated Mallitalytics collection and follow useful work into stable, browsable reading paths.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Nothing (first phase)
**Requirements**: PUB-01, PUB-02, PUB-03, EDIT-01, CUR-01, CUR-02, CUR-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A visitor can use the home page to reach featured analysis, recent work, selected visual work, and an intentional archive path.
  2. A visitor can open a stable, shareable detail page for every published launch item, including articles, cards, studies, projects, trackers, and selected social items.
  3. A visitor can browse the curated collection through small, editorially meaningful content-type archives, without being offered sparse or unsupported taxonomy pages.
  4. Each featured analytical item makes clear what to notice, the evidence and context behind it, and what to watch next.
  5. The author has a launch inventory that records each source item's publication disposition and the metadata required before it can appear publicly.

**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 2: Responsible visual and editorial context
**Goal**: Readers can use Mallitalytics visual and utility work with the context needed to understand evidence quickly and responsibly.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 1
**Requirements**: EDIT-02, EDIT-03, VIS-01
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A visitor can quickly find or act on useful baseball information in utility content without encountering a manufactured analytical thesis.
  2. A reader of analytical work can see the applicable data window, source or methodology, and a caveat whenever those details are needed to interpret the claim responsibly.
  3. A visitor on a mobile device can read a visual card or chart with descriptive alt text and context-rich captioning.
  4. A visitor can enlarge a visual or open a dedicated visual view whenever the original reading surface is insufficient.
**Plans**: TBD
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 3: Manual editorial publishing
**Goal**: The author can independently turn reviewed work into safely controlled public publication without editing website code.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 2
**Requirements**: PUB-05, STU-01, STU-02, STU-03
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. The author can create, edit, and preview a specific revision of a content item through a private studio without editing website code.
  2. The author can add or select a visual asset and record its alt text, caption, source, and method notes before publication.
  3. The author can explicitly publish, unpublish, archive, and correct an item while preserving revisions and preventing drafts from appearing publicly.
  4. A public visitor sees an item only after its public status is set and its scheduled publication time has arrived.
**Plans**: TBD
**UI hint**: yes

### Phase 4: Discovery, trust, and launch readiness
**Goal**: Visitors can find, share, and assess Mallitalytics as a brand-led, evidence-conscious baseball publication.
**Mode:** mvp
**Depends on**: Phase 3
**Requirements**: PUB-04, EDIT-04, VIS-02
**Success Criteria** (what must be TRUE):
  1. A visitor can find published work by title or controlled metadata, with usable archive filters when a direct match is unavailable.
  2. A visitor can find brand-led About, methodology, corrections, contact, and non-betting editorial-position information without encountering a résumé-style experience.
  3. Every published detail page has a canonical URL and accurate social-preview metadata for direct sharing.
  4. The launch collection provides a complete path from discovery to reading, visual evidence, archives, and editorial trust information.

**UI hint**: yes
